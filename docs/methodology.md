## 🎯 Project Overview

This portfolio project analyzes **104 industrial equipment tenders** (2022-2025) across three B2B industries (Sugar, Power Generation, Pulp & Paper) — with deep focus on **Indonesian sugar industry equipment supply**.

**Core question:** What separates winning tenders from losing ones — and how can data systematically improve win rate?

**Business context:** Tender preparation in B2B equipment supply involves balancing technical specs, pricing, lead time, and competitive positioning. Most companies make these decisions based on intuition. This project shows what happens when you bring data into the loop.

**Result:** 17 strategic insights organized across 4 analytical layers — from industry-level patterns down to regional pricing strategy implications.

---

## 👤 Why I Built This

I'm Abdurrahman — an **Applicant Engineer with 4+ years of real B2B tender experience**, currently transitioning into data analytics.

Throughout my career preparing technical & commercial proposals for sugar mills, paper plants, and power generation facilities, I noticed a pattern:

> Most tender decisions are made on gut feel.
> Yet every tender generates rich data — pricing, lead time, competitor patterns, win/loss reasons — that goes unanalyzed.

This project is what happens when you take that data and ask the right questions.

**My unique angle:** I'm not a data analyst learning B2B. I'm a **B2B specialist learning data**. The analytical methodology came from 4+ years of preparing 100+ real tenders. Every insight here connects to real strategic decisions a sales team makes weekly.

## 💡 Top Strategic Insights — Highlights

### 1. Sugar Industry = Strategic Specialization
- **73% win rate** in sugar industry, dominating 54% of total volume
- **56 tender records** in Sugar across 4 Java/Sumatra regions
- **Action:** Position company as Sugar Industry Specialist

### 2. Power Generation = Hidden Champion
- **75% win rate** (highest!) but only 23% of volume
- Concentrated in Jakarta + West Java + South Sumatra
- **Action:** Strategic growth opportunity if scaled carefully

### 3. Pricing Is the #1 Battleground
- **78% of all losses** = "Higher Price" reason
- Cuts across all industries, all regions
- **Action:** Renegotiate principal pricing for price-sensitive markets

### 4. Spare Parts = Untapped Quick Win
- **100% win rate** (10/10 tenders won)
- Lowest tender value but consistent margin (~22%)
- **Action:** Push spare parts as relationship-builder

### 5. Material Handling = Hidden Problem (43.8% WR)
- Significantly below average win rate
- Root cause: Pricing pressure + Tender Specs mismatch
- **Action:** Strategic review or selective bidding

### 6. Regional Specialization Pattern 🆕
- 6 of 8 regions have **100% single-industry concentration**
- Mirrors real Indonesian industrial geography
- **Action:** "Geographic expansion = Industry expansion"

### 7. Hidden Winner Regions 🆕
- **West Java 88%, East Java 86%** — top win rates, mid-volume
- Lampung (largest volume, 17 tenders) only 65%
- **Action:** Replicate East Java's win formula to underperformers

### 8. Riau Outlier Investigation 🆕
- **50% win rate** — single underperformer below 58%
- Pure Pulp & Paper market, price-sensitive
- **Action:** Pricing strategy review specific to Pulp market

### 9. Strategic Selectivity Trend
- Volume showed **Strategic Selectivity** pattern
- Win rate **+13.1% improvement** over 4 years
- Q3-2023 peak (85.7%) = benchmark to replicate
